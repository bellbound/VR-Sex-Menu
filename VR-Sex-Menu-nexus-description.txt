[center][color=#ffd966][b][size=5]INTRODUCTION[/size][/b][/color][/center]
[quote][b]OStim Standalone VR only gives you a menu for scenes you take part in yourself. This mod gives you the same control over scenes between NPCs - start them, swap the animation while they run, and end them whenever you like.[/b][/quote]


[center][color=#ffd966][size=5][b]KEY FEATURES[/b][/size][/color][/center]

[color=#ffe599][size=4][b]◈ Control Any Running Scene[/b][/size][/color]
[size=4]Open a scene browser for any OStim scene happening near you and change the animation or end it. Works for scenes you are part of as well.[/size]

[color=#ffe599][size=4][b]◈ Start Scenes Between NPCs[/b][/size][/color]
[size=4]Pick the participants from a UI Extensions menu and the scene starts right there.[/size]

[color=#ffe599][size=4][b]◈ Open It However You Like[/b][/size][/color]
[size=4]Cast the lesser power at any NPC, or grab one with a hand and pull the trigger on the other. Running scenes can also be reached by activating a participant or with a hotkey, both set up in the MCM. The spell can be removed from your inventory in the MCM if you only use the other options.[/size]

[color=#ffe599][size=4][b]◈ Put The Menu Where You Want It[/b][/size][/color]
[size=4]Grab the glowing orb and move the menu anywhere around you.[/size]

[color=#ffe599][size=4][b]◈ Undress And Redress[/b][/size][/color]
[size=4]Take an NPC's clothes off and put them back on straight from the menu.[/size]

[color=#ffe599][size=4][b]◈ Matching Animations For Every Body[/b][/size][/color]
[size=4]TNG and SOS futa characters are recognised and get both male and female animations.[/size]

[color=#ffe599][size=4][b]◈ Creatures[/b][/size][/color]
[size=4]Creature filtering can be turned off if you want everything available.[/size]
